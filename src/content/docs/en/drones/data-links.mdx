## Telemetry Radios

ESP-Now based (Mesh support):
- The EOLab [dronebridge-radio](https://github.com/EOLab-HSRW/dronebridge-radio).

2.4 GHz:
- [J.Fi Wireless Telemetry Module](https://jmarple.ai/j-fi/) (Harley: can't find the firmware or reference schematic)

LoRa-based
- [Dronee ZOON 2.4GHz Long Range Lora Telemetry](https://dronee.aero/collections/uav-components/products/zoon-long-range-telemetry-module)
- [Robsense SwarmLink (433 MHz)](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/common-telemetry-robsense-swarmlink.html)

840-845MHz Band:
- [CUAV P8 Radio Drone Telemetry](https://store.cuav.net/shop/p8-radio/)

Microhard Pico Serial (Microhard's proprietary radio protocol):
- [P2400 - 2.4 GHz](https://microhardcorp.com/P2400.php)
  - [ARK Electron Microhard Serial Telemetry Radios (2.4 GHz)](https://arkelectron.com/product/1w-2400mhz-serial-telemetry-radio/)
- [P900 - 900 MHz](https://microhardcorp.com/P900.php)
  - [ARK Electron Microhard Serial Telemetry Radios (900 MHz)](https://arkelectron.com/product/1w-900mhz-serial-telemetry-air-radio/)
  - [Holybro Microhard Telemetry Radio (900 MHz)](https://holybro.com/products/microhard-radio)
  - [Holybro Microhard Telemetry Radio V2 (900 MHz)](https://holybro.com/products/microhard-telemetry-radio-copy)
- [P400 - 400 MHz](https://microhardcorp.com/P400.php) (no mesh support in this frequency)
  - [Holybro Microhard Telemetry Radio V2 (400 MHz)](https://holybro.com/products/microhard-telemetry-radio-copy)

Multiples band support:
- [Mesh Rider Radios by Doodlelabs](https://doodlelabs.com/products/)
