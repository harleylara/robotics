## Exploration

In a more "formal"/"rigorous" definition of a swarm, control is decentralized.

But in practice, there are different levels of control:
- Centralized control: There is a master (usually a ground control station) commanding the position and actions of each member of the swarm.
- Hybrid control: There are one or a few manually controlled members of the swarm, and the rest “autonomously” react to these actions, in a similar fashion to [Follow the leader (game)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Follow_the_leader_(game)).
- Decentralized control: **All** members of the swarm are self-contained control units that take individual actions.

Take this as a reference, this is not meant to be a formal or academic definition of the "levels of control". The main purpose is to set the tone of the discussion topic and create a categorization to expand on in the following sections.
